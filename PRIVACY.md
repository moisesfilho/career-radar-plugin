# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

Career Radar is an open-source ChatGPT plugin skill maintained by Moises Filho.

## Information handled

Career Radar may process information that a user voluntarily provides to ChatGPT, including a resume, career summary, target roles, skills, location, work preferences, salary preferences, and scheduling preferences. It may also process publicly available job listing information returned by the host's web search.

## How information is used

The information is used only to search for relevant job opportunities, evaluate compatibility with the user's stated profile, identify gaps, and produce a career radar report. Career Radar does not apply to jobs, contact recruiters, or submit personal information on a user's behalf.

## Storage and sharing

The current version is skill-only and does not operate a separate backend, database, analytics service, or tracking system. Career Radar does not independently store or sell user information. Resume data, task history, and conversation history remain subject to the privacy practices and retention controls of the platform hosting the skill, including ChatGPT.

Career Radar does not intentionally send profile information to third parties beyond the services used by the host to execute the user's request. Search results are obtained from publicly available sources.

## Future changes

A future version may add an optional MCP server for persistent history and deduplication. Any such change will be documented before release, including what information is stored, why it is stored, and how users can request deletion.
